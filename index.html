Time

  • The exam is 180 minutes (3 hours). The timer starts when you tap Start Test and the first question appears.
  • When the timer reaches zero, the test is submitted automatically.

Marking scheme

  • +1 for each correct answer.
  • −0.25 (¼) for each incorrect answer.
  • 0 for questions you do not attempt.

Answering questions

  • Each page shows one question. Use Next and Previous to move. There is no scrolling between questions.
  • Clear Response removes your selected option. Mark for Review flags a question to revisit.
  • The question palette shows all 200 questions. Tap a number to jump to it.
1Not visited 2Not answered 3Answered 4Marked for review 5Answered & marked

Cheating rules

  • If you change tabs or leave the test window more than 3 times, you are disqualified and the test is submitted automatically.
  • You get a warning on each of the first 3 switches. The 4th switch ends the test.
  • Right-click, copy and developer-tool shortcuts are blocked and recorded. Leaving full-screen is also recorded.
  • Your cheating status is shown on your result and sent to the admin.

After the test

  • Your performance page opens right after submission. Download your performance with the answer key as a PDF to see which questions were right or wrong.
  • Write your doubt or feedback about the test (required), then tap Share with Admin. Your result and doubt are sent together on WhatsApp.
  • Admin WhatsApp number: 8116217554. Please share your result here. The admin cannot see or delete results from your chats.
  • The leaderboard will be released on the next day.
